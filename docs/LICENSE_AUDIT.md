# OSKA PLAY — Legal & Licensing Compliance Audit

---

## 1. Compliance Principles
OSKA PLAY adheres to intellectual property laws, open source licenses, and digital rights regulations:
1. **Zero DRM Circumvention**: The platform never decrypts, unpacks, or modifies proprietary commercial games protected by digital locks.
2. **Authentic Runtimes**: The platform never fabricates fake WebAssembly runtimes or presents simulated gameplay.
3. **Explicit Permissions Required**: No game is published to the public playable catalog unless its source code and content assets are verified under lawful open-source or permissive redistribution terms.
